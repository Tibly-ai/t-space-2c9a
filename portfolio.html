Selected Works

An exploration of space defined by monolithic geometry, regional masonry, and the deliberate choreography of daylight. Each entry represents a unique response to site topography and local climate.

Project 01 / Desert Oasis

The Quarry House

Sited inside an inactive limestone quarry, this volume is cast from locally sourced aggregates, creating an absolute continuation of the canyon walls.

Location West Texas, USA
Completed 2023
Project 02 / Coastal Shelter

The Driftwood Pavilion

A low-profile coastal retreat utilizing charred cedar siding and raw structural steel to withstand maritime wind loads while preserving sweeping horizon views.

Location Oregon Coast
Completed 2024
Project 03 / Cultural

The Void Gallery

A small-scale exhibition hall oriented entirely toward the movement of sunlight. Roof apertures throw linear shadows across structural white concrete walls.

Location Santa Fe, New Mexico
Completed 2022
